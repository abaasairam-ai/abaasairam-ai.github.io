GST portal consolidator — a run that survives a logout

Collects the TDS and TCS credit received on the GST portal across a full financial year, one month at a time, and writes a consolidated workbook. A human handles the login; the tool handles the twelve months of clicking.

What is real here

  • Every line of console output — taken from the source
  • The checkpoint-after-each-month and resume behaviour
  • The one-financial-year-per-run scope limit and its stated reason
  • The workbook columns, including amendments as negative rows

What is invented

  • All deductor names, GSTINs and amounts
  • The row counts per month
  • The output filename

ResilienceThe session expiry above is the point of this screen. The portal logs you out mid-run. Because every month is checkpointed as it completes, the run resumes from August rather than restarting from April. Losing four months of work to a timeout is what makes people give up on a tool.
ScopeOne financial year per run, deliberately. The docstring says it plainly: safer than ninety months in one session. A deliberate scope cut, written down with its reason, is worth more than an ambitious version that fails at month forty.

Reading this honestly

  • This is a reconstruction of a terminal session. The real tool needs Playwright and a live portal login with CAPTCHA and OTP entered by hand.
  • The session expiry is scripted here, but it is a real failure mode and handling it is why the checkpointing exists.
  • Tax collected at source is skipped deliberately — another scope cut with a reason attached.
