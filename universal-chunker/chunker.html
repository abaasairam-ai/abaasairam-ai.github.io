How to read the results. The strategy column shows how the document was understood. markdown-headings and numbered-sections mean real structure was found and used. none-plain-prose means none was found, so it fell back to splitting on paragraphs and whole sentences — still safe, just less precise. needs OCR means the PDF is a scan with no text layer; run OCR on it first rather than trusting anything extracted.
