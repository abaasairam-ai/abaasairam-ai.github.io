About

Background

I'm Sairam, a financial accountant in Hyderabad, India, leading a team of four across full-cycle accounting, payroll, GST/TDS, audits and regulatory filings — CA (Inter) qualified.

What sets my work apart is that I don't just run finance processes — I automate them. Self-taught in Python and AI tooling, I build my own tools rather than buying software: JSON generators for tax filings, reconciliation engines, document-parsing pipelines, and private AI assistants that keep sensitive data in-house.

I care about the unglamorous part. Most AI-in-finance demos fall over on real documents — a table split across pages, a section number formatted inconsistently, a confident answer with no citation. Getting those right is the actual work, and it's where a finance background beats a purely technical one: I know what a wrong number costs.
